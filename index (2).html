How the two days work

A relaxed pace, with a tutor beside you at every step.

Day 1: Shape it

  • Meet the team and the tools
  • Measure and mark your boards
  • Cut the top, legs and shelf
  • Dry-fit your table

Day 2: Make it yours

  • Drill and screw the joints
  • Sand to a smooth finish
  • Oil or stain your table
  • Take it home

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

No. All levels are welcome and we guide you through every step.

What is included in the price?

All materials, tools and guidance. You keep the table you make.

What should I wear?

Closed shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dusty. We provide safety gear.

Do I have to attend both days?

Yes. The table is built over both afternoons, so please book both.

How do I reserve a seat?

Tap Book your spot on WhatsApp. Seats are limited and the page shows how many are open.
